# Story V-009: Player Damage + Respawn (Lite)

> **Status**: In Progress
> **Type**: Logic
> **Estimated**: 1h
> **Depends on**: V-002 (player), V-008 (enemy hurt source)
> **Unlocks**: combat loop closure

## Goal

The full V-009 in INDEX called for 16 HP, knockback, and checkpoint
restoration. For the v0.2 demo we ship a **lite** version: 1 HP, instant
respawn at the player's spawn position, and ~1 second of invincibility
during which the player blinks and cannot take a second hit. Full HP/
knockback/checkpoint flow is deferred to a later milestone.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] New tunable on `Player`:
  - [ ] `@export var invincibility_duration: float = 1.0`
- [ ] New internal state:
  - [ ] `_spawn_position: Vector2` — captured in `_ready()` from initial `global_position`
  - [ ] `_invincible_timer: float = 0.0`
  - [ ] `_hurt_detector: Area2D` — programmatic Area2D child, `collision_mask = 4` (Enemy layer), 12×16 rectangle collider centred on player
- [ ] `_ready()` builds `_hurt_detector` and connects `body_entered` to `_on_enemy_hit`
- [ ] `_on_enemy_hit(body)` — short-circuits on `Game.test_mode` OR `_invincible_timer > 0.0`. If `body is Enemy and body.is_alive`, calls `respawn()`.
- [ ] `respawn()` — public method. Sets `global_position = _spawn_position`, zeroes velocity, arms `_invincible_timer = invincibility_duration`.
- [ ] `_physics_process` decays `_invincible_timer` and toggles `visible` on a sin-wave blink while invincible.

## Out of Scope

- Knockback velocity on hit (deferred)
- Checkpoint flags (deferred — respawn always uses the initial spawn position)
