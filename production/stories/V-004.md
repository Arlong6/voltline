# Story V-004: Dash — Tap C, ~14 Frames of Horizontal Burst, No Air-Dash

> **Status**: In Progress
> **Type**: Logic
> **Estimated**: 1.5–2h
> **Depends on**: V-002, V-003 (consumes `facing`)
> **Unlocks**: V-005 (wall slide / wall jump uses dash for distance)

## Goal

Tap **C** (or **Shift**) on the ground to dash horizontally for ~14 frames at
~2.4× walk speed in the current facing direction. Dash locks facing and
overrides the walk velocity for its duration. No air-dash (must be grounded
to initiate). Dash continues if the player jumps or walks off a ledge mid-
dash, but cannot reverse direction.

  3. **Horizontal velocity** — `if _dash_timer > 0.0: velocity.x = facing * dash_speed; else: velocity.x = input_x * walk_speed`
  4. Gravity, timer decay (incl. `_dash_timer`), jump trigger, variable jump cutoff (unchanged from V-002)

## Implementation Notes

- "No air-dash" enforced by the `is_grounded` check at trigger. Once a dash
  is active, it continues regardless of grounded state — so jump-out-of-dash
  keeps the boosted horizontal speed (the X8 dash-jump technique).
- Dash overrides walk by virtue of being checked first in step 3, with the
  walk branch as the `else`. Pressing the opposite direction during a dash
  has no effect because facing was locked in step 1.
- No explicit dash cooldown — the duration itself gates re-triggering. Once
  `_dash_timer` reaches 0 the player can dash again on the next press.
- We deliberately keep gravity active during dash. With our flat floor this
  is invisible; once V-007 introduces ledges, the dash arc will look right.
